Welcome to Food Scanner

Your personal nutrition assistant

Food Scanner helps you make informed food choices by providing detailed nutritional information from barcodes. This app analyzes product ingredients, calculates health scores, and offers personalized recommendations for healthier alternatives.

📷

Barcode Scanning

Quickly scan food product barcodes with your camera or enter codes manually

🔍

Nutrition Facts

Get detailed nutritional information including calories, fat, protein, carbs, and more

📊

Health Ratings

See at-a-glance health, nutrition, and fat scores with color-coded indicators

📚

Educational Resources

Learn about nutrition basics, food labels, healthy eating tips, and seasonal foods

📱

Offline Access

Install as a PWA for offline access and faster performance

🔄

Scan History

Keep track of previously scanned items for quick reference
